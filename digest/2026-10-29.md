## 🏆 Top 3

### 1. Mozilla announces enterprise add-on policies for AMO, launching December 1

Mozilla says enterprise add-ons will launch on addons.mozilla.org on December 1, 2026. It cites input from enterprise organizations that distribute add-ons strictly via Firefox, and introduces enterprise add-on policies.

**Why it matters to you:** Add-on store policy changes are on your list under browser extensions, and this one comes with a firm date.

[Mozilla Add-ons blog](https://blog.mozilla.org/addons/2026/10/28/introducing-enterprise-add-on-policies/)

### 2. Revolut reports data from 2,000 engineers on AI coding tools and self-hosted models

David Abitbol, who leads AI operations at Revolut, shares data from 2,000 engineers on what AI tools did to coding time and lead time. He says self-hosting open-source GLM models made the biggest difference: about 60-70% faster delivery at roughly a tenth of what they were paying frontier labs.

**Why it matters to you:** Numbers on how an agent workflow was measured across a large team fit your interest in evals and agentic coding practice.

[AI Engineer (YouTube)](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Ok6QdZHCcI8)

### 3. kw-bench: a benchmark for breaking frontier coding-agent harnesses on real-world tasks

The paper notes that inside a harness (a loop, a shell, a filesystem, the ability to write and run code) a model can take on increasingly difficult work. It treats the coding-agent harness as infrastructure and introduces kw-bench.

**Why it matters to you:** Harness design and how to evaluate it are at the top of your list.

[arXiv](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.36405)
