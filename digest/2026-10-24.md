## 🏆 Top 3

### 1. Anthropic donates the Agent Skills standard to the Agentic AI Foundation

Simon Willison relays news from Anthropic: it says it is committed to keeping Agent Skills open, community-driven and vendor-neutral, and is donating the standard to the Agentic AI Foundation.

**Why it matters to you:** Skills are on your Claude Code list; this concerns who stewards the format you build against.

[Simon Willison](https://simonwillison.net/2026/Oct/22/donating-agent-skills/)

### 2. Anthropic guide argues most agents need fewer subagents

A Reddit post says Anthropic published an 18-minute guide on Oct 22, 'When to fold subagents into your main agent loop'. Per the post, it favours one main loop with skills and tool search, and mentions cutting Claude Code's system prompt by over 80%.

**Why it matters to you:** Subagent and harness design is your top interest. This is a Reddit summary, so check the original guide.

[Reddit](https://www.reddit.com/r/ClaudeAI/comments/1xdwquj/anthropics_new_guide_oct_22_argues_most_agents/)

### 3. Kimi K3.1 Pro lands on the Kimi API, weights promised by Nov 4

Per Simon Willison, Kimi K3.1 Pro is available on the Kimi API but not yet open weights. Moonshot promises full model weights will be released by Nov 4, 2026.

**Why it matters to you:** Other labs' releases get one line in your list; this is here because it is a major release drawing heavy discussion on Hacker News.

[Simon Willison](https://simonwillison.net/2026/Oct/23/kimi-k31-pro/)
